Making a Career Shift Into Product Management: Vasudevan Kumar’s Simplilearn Review

Vasudevan Kumar’s story, as documented by him

Vasudevan Kumar has over 12 years of professional experience across industries, including five years in IT. After working in IT, business development, project management, and entrepreneurship, he wanted more clarity on his next move and chose product management. He enrolled in Simplilearn’s Professional Certification in Product Management, associated with SP Jain. He now works as an Associate Product Manager at Mobiux Labs.

Where Vasudevan’s Journey Began: A Varied Career Path

Vasudevan completed his bachelor’s degree in Information Technology in 2010 and started that year as an Administrator at XP Infotech. He later worked as a Customer Support Engineer at SKF India, a Consultant – Data and Processing at Ericsson, and a Front-end Developer at ATOM Systems. In 2019 he started his own advertising agency. In 2021 he joined Ideaedu Technologies as a Project Manager, leading a team of eight across design, development, testing, and DevOps, and in January 2023 he joined Mobiux Labs.

Why Vasudevan Decided to Upskill in Product Management

Vasudevan felt product management matched his experience and future goals, but he wanted to understand the role more thoroughly and build the strategic and technical knowledge to enter the field. After researching what companies expect from product managers, he chose a certification program.

Choosing the Best Product Management Course: Why Vasudevan Picked Simplilearn

For professionals comparing product management courses, his approach is a useful checklist: review the curriculum, look for practical projects, and consider the academic association. Vasudevan chose Simplilearn after finding that the curriculum covered skills and projects relevant to product roles. The program’s association with SP Jain was another factor.

He rates his experience highly, especially the course structure and instructor-led sessions. Repeated sessions helped him understand concepts from different angles. Design thinking, empathy mapping, and idea generation stood out, and the course strengthened an area he wanted to improve: strategic planning, including product strategy, pricing strategy, and the technical side of product management.

Moving Into an Associate Product Manager Role

Vasudevan began applying for product roles halfway through the course. After completing the program, he joined Mobiux Labs as an Associate Product Manager, a move into a new domain. He reports that the transition came with a salary increase. In his current role he handles two products, builds modules, and develops delivery roadmaps, applying concepts from the program, particularly product and pricing strategy.

Looking Ahead

Over the next five years, Vasudevan hopes to start his own business and launch a product. He already has several ideas and works on them in his free time, including content ideas and building his own brand. Outside work, he enjoys audiobooks.

What Vasudevan’s Story Means for Career Changers

Structured learning can help experienced professionals find a new direction, build relevant skills, and prepare for a career transition.
